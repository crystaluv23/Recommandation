## New skill and its justification

`order_confirmation_disclosed:v3` has the same spec except version and exactly
the same fixture bytes as v2. The new skill adds a ceiling-only award restriction
requiring a minimum or expected value, plus explicit neutral handling of
non-qualifying distribution/joint-pursuit arrangements. All eight inherited
fixtures passed live admission; none exercises the newly added ceiling-only rule
or either specific arrangement.

The original Update rationale treats `counter` citations supporting a neutral
result as if they asserted a negative measurement state. Case-004's own
diagnostics instead correctly call its neutral result source-supported. Its
Master Distribution and Joint Pursuit Agreement spans do not, by themselves,
prove an execution defect or a predictive benefit from changing the skill.
Evidence role, measured state and association with a surge label are separate.

Case-003's award explicitly contains a $2M minimum alongside a $3.4B maximum.
It therefore does not establish a ceiling-only false positive. The fixed
definition supplies materiality thresholds and permits stated/implied value,
volume or delivery schedule; the new restriction's semantic equivalence remains
unverified. This audit does not silently choose a new definition or reverse the
published version. An additional rule needs source-grounded justification and
representative counterexamples; passing untouched old fixtures cannot supply it.

2024-12-31 23:59:59 UTC; its literal provider record also says
`time_published=20241231T235959`. The issuer's original announcement is dated
2024-05-09, with matching amount, product, country and shipment year.
[Issuer announcement PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_2c0d3a3bb1837dda98b42c06def0bd22/energyrecovery/news/2024-05-09_Energy_Recovery_Announces_15_Million_in_SWRO_348.pdf).

For the actual induction cutoff 2025-01-31, the skill's 180-day lookback begins
2024-08-04. The original announcement is outside that window. Execution `594`
explicitly calls the December date in-window and returns positive using the
order summary; Update `599` then describes that positive measurement as
misaligned with the control label in `update-case-001`. This is a proven
stale-source clock failure contaminating a learning input, not a proven
future-data leak. Removing that source does not determine the correct state for
the remaining corpus, and does not authorize rewriting the historical result.
