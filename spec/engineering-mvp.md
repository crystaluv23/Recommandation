# Fast/Slow engineering MVP acceptance

Status: adopted 2026-10-19 from the user's supplied acceptance standard; none of
the criteria below declares the system passed. This is a bounded engineering
milestone, separate from statistical prediction improvement and independent
evolution benefit. Existing later research objectives remain unresolved.

## Supported scope and evidence

Current acceptance covers the repository's declared InformationPlan actions,
versioned Registry Signal execution types and locally available provider source
shapes, within a frozen case/time/source/Plan/Registry/context/budget envelope.
No universal future-input correctness is claimed. Unsupported actions are
rejected; missing data, conflicting data, inapplicability and technical failure
must stay distinct. A limited search permits only a bounded not-found statement.

Each stage's acceptance record must freeze: input conditions and source/version/
permission/time requirements; correct output meaning; insufficient-input
behavior; forbidden inference or deletion; an independent original-material
oracle; downstream persistence and exact consumption; original failure,
representative variant, counterexample and answerable positive control; and
which evidence layer is required for closure. The oracle is not supplied to
the role under evaluation. Corrections stay within the original typed authority
and frozen call ceiling. Report first answer, corrected acceptance and final
failure separately, preserving the complete frozen failure denominator.

Direction, negative/counterevidence and magnitude are all required capabilities.
Non-surge is not absence of a positive Signal. Magnitude features must be
measurable at prediction time. Natural no-pattern is legal after sufficient
inspection but cannot replace answerable Mining→admission→reuse positive
controls for both direction and magnitude. Handwritten seeded Signals only
verify the later lifecycle, never Mining capability.

## Frozen batches and close condition

Freeze samples/denominator; code, model/backend, Plan, Registry and source
versions; independent answers and allowed uncertainty; failure conditions;
initial/correction/cost limits; and repair versus generalization samples before
new role outputs. Cover answerable, negative, missing, partial, object/period/
unit mismatch, contaminated historical body, legal future forecast, long/page/
budget, source conflict, version/reuse and interruption/idempotency families.

All predeclared must-pass samples must achieve the correct result, including
positive controls, with no known critical semantic error accepted. Safe
rejection is necessary but cannot replace normal capability. Verify downstream
meaning/version and restart behavior, then one actual Fast→Slow→successor Fast
engineering loop. Smaller known blockers are resolved before that whole-loop
check. Existing 80% stable-ref data readiness, diagnostic Binary F1 ≥0.60 and
completion pointers keep their original meanings and cannot replace semantics.

Evidence grows from original-material review and deterministic checks, to
production-entry local regressions, independent fresh role execution, actual
downstream consumption and a real dual-loop run with necessary recovery.
Local independent subagents may replace API roles under the user's zero-API
budget, through exact production request/response handoff. Such results identify
the actual backend, retain raw requests/tools/answers/corrections/accepted or
rejected states and consumers, and do not establish remote-provider behavior.
Historical replay, mocks, scripted answers and file existence are weaker
evidence. Independent prediction benefit is outside this engineering close.

The first frozen seven-case Reflect batch is generated by
[the local role acceptance driver](scripts/run_local_role_acceptance.md). It
establishes one stage's evidence, not the whole MVP or source authenticity.
The original two samples retain their known contaminated upstream input
limitation; five synthetic contracts have explicitly synthetic evidence.

Proceed in order: trusted inputs → retrieval/read fidelity → Signal meaning →
Reflect/final context → Slow discovery/admission → exact successor use →
recovery/whole-loop. Current source authenticity, Reflect semantics, Slow
feedback consumption and magnitude discovery/reuse remain open.
