# Technical Failure Before Quality Attribution

visit_times: 1

## Trigger

When a frozen evolution run loses F1, excludes cases, or rejects a mined factor at
a correction, materialization, fixture, or capability boundary.

## Lesson

Replay the exact persisted frontier before attributing the outcome to research or
factor quality. A transport no-progress guard may correctly block a duplicate
request while still needing to hand the existing typed invalid object to a bounded
deterministic fallback. Likewise, a proposal that mixes execution modalities is a
capability/split gap, not evidence that its economic hypothesis failed.

## Context

V141 counted five structured-correction failures in the frozen F1 denominator.
Two stopped at the identical-request guard even though the typed ResearchState
frontier was mechanically removable; three same-Signal quantity frontiers exposed
source-coordinate dependency paths that disappeared with the authorized evidence
row. Separately, `disclosed_order_value_to_revenue_ratio` mixed narrative order
disclosure with a structured revenue denominator, but was materialized as a pure
narrative Signal and appeared to fail eight fixtures. Exact request/response replay
separated these engineering outcomes from model and factor quality.

## How to Apply

1. Keep technical failures in the frozen score denominator, but isolate them from
   research attribution.
2. Replay the last typed invalid object and obligations with no network calls.
3. Let duplicate-request prevention stop transport only; if a contract-approved
   deterministic fallback exists, run it and fully revalidate the result.
4. Classify candidate modality before materialization. Persist mixed candidates as
   `capability_gap/split_required` and exclude them from factor-effectiveness verdicts.
5. Compare model/factor quality only after the same frozen cohort completes through
   the repaired engineering path.

## Limits

Do not use fallback to invent replacement evidence, relax validation, remove an
unlisted dependency, or rescore away failures. A mixed proposal is not accepted; it
must return as separately executable candidates and pass the ordinary fixture,
holdout, paired, and promotion gates.
