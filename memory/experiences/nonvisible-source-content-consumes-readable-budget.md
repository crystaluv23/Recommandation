# Nonvisible Source Content Consumes the Readable Budget

visit_times: 4

## Trigger

Changing filing selection/projection/disclosure, or investigating valid-looking
contexts whose claims disagree with available financial sources.

## Lesson

Strip nonvisible source blocks before selecting readable windows. Removing them
only after selecting raw snippets cannot recover a budget already spent inside
an enormous hidden header; a clipped snippet may lack its closing tag. Selection,
projection and source directories must share data-owned visibility with exact raw
lineage. A title/family fetch is not a body read, and a fragment miss is not absence.
A directory needs usable body/section coordinates within the actual read budget.

## Context and limits

The immutable v162 Enbridge filing had all 18 selected windows inside a 512k XBRL
header. Equal-budget source replay exposed financial statements after the fix.
The real Slow wire also revealed that its controller adapter bypassed the common
identity scope. Verify actual composed/wire requests and inverse tool handles,
not just titles or isolated view helpers. See ../../reports/v163-source-boundary-replay-20261001.json.
These checks prove coverage, lineage and boundary wiring, not semantic completeness
or a prediction gain. Keep the fixed cohort and test the actual successor.
