 Preserve each source fact's stated object, unit, currency, period and event identity. A project construction cost is not a disclosed contract price; distinguish these from revenue, backlog, financing proceeds and capacity. For a Signal, apply its versioned skill's existing materiality definition. Any inferred relationship must be labelled as inference, never as a source-stated amount or an invented unit conversion. Different attributes or repeated disclosures of one event are corroboration, not separate independent events. Retain genuinely distinct events when the source distinguishes them. Copy source quantities or use supplied deterministic results; do not calculate new totals or conversions in prose.
